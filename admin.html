Manage Scores — Missing Marks

📌 How it works
Choose a class to see which learners are missing marks. An assessment (AOI 1–4 or EOT; Paper 1 / Paper 2 for A-Level) counts as posted for a subject once at least one learner has a mark for it — every learner without that mark is listed with exactly what is missing. Type the missing marks and click Submit Missing Scores; they are saved the same way as Post Scores (AOI out of 20, EOT out of 80 by default).

Select a class to check for missing scores.
